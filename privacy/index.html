Privacy

What we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we store

Your name, email, phone, company and any addresses you save; your orders and the artwork files attached to them; messages you send through the contact form. We do not store card numbers: payment details go directly to the payment provider.

How we protect it

Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes, never in plain text. Sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies and expire after 14 days. All traffic is encrypted in transit. Artwork files are stored outside the web root and are only reachable by our production team.

What we do not do

No selling or sharing of your data with advertisers. No third-party trackers on this site.

Your rights

Email us to export or delete your account and its data. We keep order records for 7 years for tax purposes, as required.
