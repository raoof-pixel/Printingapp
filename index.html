How an order works

  1. Choose a material and a stock size. The preview on each product page draws your size to scale.
  2. Add finishing. Sides, grommets, holes, hems, contour cutting, rush. Each add-on shows its price before you add it.
  3. Upload print-ready artwork. PDF, AI, JPG, PNG or TIFF with fonts outlined. Add it on the product page or at checkout.
  4. Pay and track. Secure card payment at checkout. Lead times start when we confirm your art.
