About this material

Two sheets of aluminum bonded to a solid polyethylene core. Dead flat, rust-free and rigid enough to span a storefront. Printed direct and protected with matte or gloss lamination; dry-erase lamination available on request. Can be contour cut and drilled for mounting.

Good for

  • Storefront fascia
  • Real-estate and development signs
  • Parking and safety panels
  • Menu boards
