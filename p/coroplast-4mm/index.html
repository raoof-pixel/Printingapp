About this material

Corrugated plastic, 4 mm. Light enough to mail, stiff enough for a stake. Printed one or two sides. Flute direction matters for stakes: vertical flutes take H-stakes.

Good for

  • Lawn and election signs
  • Open house and directional signs
  • Event and parking signage
