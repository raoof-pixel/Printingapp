Terms

The short version: you own your artwork and are responsible for it being print-ready; we print what you send.

Artwork

By uploading a file you confirm you have the right to print it. We do not proof-read, colour-correct or fix files unless you ask and we agree a charge. Low-resolution or wrong-size files are printed as supplied.

Colour

Screen colour and print colour differ. We print to industry CMYK standards; exact Pantone matches on digital print are not guaranteed.

Lead times

Business days from file confirmation. Rush options are subject to press availability.

Cancellations and reprints

Orders can be cancelled before production starts. Once printed, custom work cannot be returned. We reprint at no charge for our own errors: wrong size, wrong material, wrong finishing, or damage before pickup.

Prices

In Canadian dollars, before HST. We can correct an obvious pricing error before accepting an order.
