Help and artwork specs

Everything you need to place an order that prints right the first time.

Artwork specs

  • Formats: PDF (preferred), AI, JPG, PNG or TIFF. Up to 40 MB per file.
  • Size: build the file at the final size you chose, plus 0.125 in bleed on every edge. For 48 × 96 in and larger, build at 50% and tell us in the notes.
  • Resolution: 150 ppi at final size is plenty for boards and banners; 300 ppi for vinyl viewed up close.
  • Fonts: outline them or embed them. We cannot substitute missing fonts.
  • Colour: CMYK. Pantone references welcome in the notes. White ink on clear materials needs a separate spot layer named White.
  • Double-sided: one file with two pages, or two files named front and back.

Sizes and flutes

Every size is listed as width × height in inches. On coroplast, flute direction decides whether an H-stake fits: vertical flutes take stakes. Where a size is only stocked one way we say so beside the size.

Pricing

The price beside each size is for one piece with the default finishing, before HST. Add-ons show their price in the dropdown before you choose them. Quantity pricing is linear; for runs over 100 pieces, ask for a quote and we will do better.

Lead times

Lead times are business days from the moment we confirm your file, not from payment. Rush options are listed per material and a few of them ask you to call first so we can promise the press time.

Accounts

You need an account to check out so you can track orders and reorder. Passwords must be 10+ characters. Six wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes.
